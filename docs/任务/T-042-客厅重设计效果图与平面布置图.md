## 为什么做

客厅（房间编号 `seance`）是灵媒拉坦特夫人待的地方：第 0、1 章玩家在这里跟她说话，第 5 章白天看她的随身物，晚上在这里开降灵会。

T-033 的 v3 效果图画的是一间约 6 × 9 m、分成三块的长厅，可客厅实际只有 **6.30 × 4.50 m**，面积只有一半。照着那张图建模，圆桌椅一组、沙发一组都要塞进这间房，结果很挤（10-18 已经重排过一次，仍然不满意）。**用户 10-18 定下：这间房放不下两组，只留沙发组**：

1. 平日只有**壁炉 + 沙发组**，保留现在“沙发正对壁炉、两把扶手椅分在炉膛两侧”的格局。
2. 沙发**不能挡住两个出入口**（东墙通门厅的门、北墙通走廊的门），两门之间要能直接走通。
3. 平日**不放圆桌椅**。降灵会要用的圆桌平时是一张**翻面桌**（桌面能竖起来的独腿圆桌），折起来靠在东墙；第 5 章晚上打开，摆在壁炉前，沙发和扶手椅挪到墙边（做成状态层，见下文）。

本任务交的是重新建模前的设计依据：两张平面布置图（平日、降灵会）+ 7 张效果图。**建模不在本卡里**，由 agent 照着交付物改 `26_room_seance.py`。旧的 v3 两张图（`seance_seance_mid-v3`、`seance_table_candle-v3`）房间比例失真，**作废**，只保留在 `review/` 里备查。

### 6. 出图方法（防止 AI 把房间画大）

上一轮失败的原因是生图模型默认画“豪宅大厅”。这次按下面做：

1. **先画两张平面布置图**，用户确认比例和位置后再出效果图（草稿检查点）。
2. **构图底图（推荐）**：在 Blender 里按平面布置图用方块摆出家具（或直接用已建好的客厅模型挪家具），从每个视角的相机渲一张白模图，作为参考图交给生图工具，要求“构图、透视、家具大小与位置严格照这张图，只换材质和光”。工具不支持参考图时，只用下面的提示词。
3. **提示词**用 `tools/art/write_v3_room_prompts.py` 的 `HEAD + STYLE`，**删掉 STYLE 里“钟停在七点”那一句**，再接上下面两段（英文原样用），最后写每个视角的画面描述（照上表“画面里必须有”）。

比例锁（每张房间视角都加）：

```text
SCALE LOCK: this is a SMALL drawing room, NOT a hall. Interior only 6.3 m wide (east-west) by 4.5 m deep (north-south), ceiling 3.3 m, so the room is almost as tall as it is deep. The south wall has exactly three white six-over-six sash windows separated by narrow piers only 0.9 m wide; the west wall has a chimney breast with the fireplace on its southern half and ONE sash window on its northern half; there are no other windows. Everything is close: from the east doorway the fireplace is only 5.5 m away and the back of the sofa only 2.5 m away. Eye-level camera at 1.55 m, 24 mm lens equivalent (about 74 degrees horizontal field of view), straight verticals, no fisheye, no wide-angle exaggeration.
```

家具锁（平日四张 + 茶几近景）：

```text
FURNITURE LOCK (ordinary day): the ONLY furniture is: one three-seat wine-red velvet sofa (1.9 m long) facing the fireplace with its back to the east door; two upholstered armchairs angled toward the fire, one on each side of the hearth; a low rectangular mahogany tea table between sofa and hearth; one faded Persian rug under this group; a standard lamp behind the south end of the sofa; a mahogany bookcase on the north wall left of the corridor door; an upright piano with stool on the north wall right of the door, a potted kentia palm between door and piano; on the east wall a folded tilt-top round table standing with its round top vertical against the wall and one side chair beside it, and a small cabinet with a horn gramophone (matte horn). A brass chandelier, two wall sconces. There is NO table in use with chairs around it, NO second sofa, NO footstool, NO writing desk, NO mirror.
```

家具锁（降灵会两张）：

```text
FURNITURE LOCK (seance night): the tilt-top round table (0.9 m) is now opened in front of the cold, unlit fireplace with five plain mahogany side chairs evenly around it; the sofa has been pushed against the south wall under the drawn curtains; one armchair stands alone about 2 m from the table, turned toward it, empty; the other armchair is pushed into a corner. All curtains drawn. The ONLY light is six lit candles in brass sticks arranged in a ring on the table around a small dark photograph; large areas of pure black. No fire in the grate, no lamps lit.
```

4. 每张图出来先对照“构图核对”表和“画面里必须有”，不合格的单张定向重出（写清哪里错，例如“壁炉太小，房间画大了”），不要整组重来。
